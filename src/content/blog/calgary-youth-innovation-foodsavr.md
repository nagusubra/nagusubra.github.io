From forgetting groceries in the fridge to building an app to save them.

This Saturday I'm sharing something close to my heart: my journey with foodsavr, a passion project to tackle food waste at home. You'll also hear from 10 other inspiring voices and meet driven, imaginative, and creative Calgary youth.

![](/posts/calgary-youth-innovation-foodsavr/img-02.jpg)

Food waste is something we all struggle with, yet it often goes unnoticed. What started as me helping my mom track groceries has grown into a project I hope can make a real impact.

Come hang out, hear our stories, and connect!
